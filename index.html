02 · Mission & Vision

Family-owned since 2011.

JMG Systems began as a full-service software and web company and refocused in 2016 on what mattered most: helping small organizations use technology well. Today it’s a small team — deliberately — serving partner organizations across Pennsylvania, most of them for many years as original clients.

Mission

We design, manage, and continually improve technology and processes for small businesses — so our partners can focus on their mission, not their systems.

Vision

Small businesses whose time, data, and resources are well-stewarded — technology quietly serving the mission, their people free to serve their clients.
